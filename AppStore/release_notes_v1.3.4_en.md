# v1.3.4 Release Notes (for App Store submission)

```
• You can now pinch to zoom in and out on the video while recording scores. Even on a small iPhone screen, you can clearly see the moment of the goal and where the ball is as you record

• While zoomed in, drag to move around the video. Double-tap to switch quickly between zoomed and normal view

• The "back 5 seconds," "forward 5 seconds," and play buttons on the score recording screen are now larger and spaced further apart, making them much harder to mis-tap
```
